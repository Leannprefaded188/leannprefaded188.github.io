🚀 What is ADR?

ADR is a powerful security tool designed for businesses that use AI agents. It helps you monitor, test, and protect your AI systems from threats like prompt injection, data leaks, and unauthorized actions. ADR gives you full visibility into how your AI agents behave, so you can catch problems before they become serious.

Originally developed for Uber's production environment, ADR is now available for you to use on your own Windows computer. Whether you're managing a team of AI assistants or just want to keep your experiments safe, ADR is the easiest way to add enterprise-grade security.

✨ Key Features

🔍 Agent Observability

See every action your AI agent takes. Logs, inputs, outputs, and decision paths are recorded in real time.

⚖️ Security Benchmarking

Test your AI agents against industry-standard security benchmarks. Know exactly how safe your system is.

🚨 Threat Detection

Automatically detect prompt injection, jailbreak attempts, and other common attacks on AI agents.
